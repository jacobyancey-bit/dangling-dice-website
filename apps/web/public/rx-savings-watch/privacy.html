Rx Savings Watch — Privacy Policy

Published by Dangling Dice Studios™ • Effective Date: November 21, 2026

Plain-English Summary: Zero Personal Data Collected

Rx Savings Watch does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data or personal health information (PHI). No account, name, email address, phone number, or insurance details are ever requested or required.

1. Information Stored Strictly on Your Device

The medications you choose to watch ("My Cabinet"), your display preferences (such as Large Text mode), and your notification settings are saved exclusively in local storage on your own phone or tablet. Dangling Dice Studios™ cannot see, access, or recover your medication list.

You may permanently erase all locally stored data at any time using the "Erase My Medication List" button in the app's Settings screen, or by uninstalling the app.

2. Anonymous Medication Catalog & Generic Update Checks

When you search the drug catalog or check for newly launched generics, biosimilars, or FDA drug shortages, the app communicates with our server using only public drug identifiers (NLM RxNorm RXCUIs):

  • No User Identifiers: Requests contain no user ID, advertising ID, device fingerprint, or account token.
  • Query Obfuscation (k-Anonymity Chaffing): Update checks mix your watched drug IDs with randomly selected common maintenance drug IDs so the server cannot infer which specific medications belong to you.
  • No Query Logging: Our server does not log IP addresses alongside drug lookup queries or build user profiles.

3. No Third-Party Trackers or Advertising SDKs

Rx Savings Watch does not include third-party advertising networks, behavioral analytics trackers, or social media tracking pixels. Notifications are generated locally on your device without transmitting your medication list to third-party push notification brokers.

4. Official Public Data Sources

Medication catalog, generic approval, pricing, and shortage data are compiled solely from publicly available U.S. government datasets, including:

  • National Library of Medicine (NLM) RxNorm
  • U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) Orange Book & Purple Book
  • OpenFDA National Drug Code (NDC) Directory & FDA Drug Shortages
  • Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) NADAC Pricing

5. Children's Privacy

Rx Savings Watch is intended for general adult informational use and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Changes & Contact

If we ever update this Privacy Policy, we will post the revised version at this URL with an updated effective date. For questions regarding privacy, please visit dangling-dice-studios.com.
