Payroll Calculator — Privacy Policy

Published by Dangling Dice Studios™ • Effective Date: November 23, 2026

Summary

Payroll Calculator (com.danglingdicestudios.payroll_calculator) is an offline-first utility app. It does not require an account or login. All payroll, shift, employee, timesheet, and mileage records stay strictly on your device. To keep the app free, banner and interstitial ads are served via Google AdMob.

1. Local-Only Payroll Data

All records you enter in Payroll Calculator — including pay profiles, hourly rates, shifts, overtime hours, employee profiles, timesheets, shift templates, and mileage logs — are stored exclusively in local storage on your device. Dangling Dice Studios™ does not collect, access, sell, or transmit your payroll or financial records to our servers.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Payroll Calculator displays banner and interstitial ads provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may automatically collect technical, non-payroll data to serve and measure ads, including:

  • Device or other identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • App interaction, crash, and performance diagnostics

Learn how Google uses data from partner apps at Google Privacy & Terms. You can reset or limit personalized ads anytime in Android under Settings → Google → Ads.

3. On-Device Pay Stub Scanning (OCR)

When you use the optional Pay Stub Scanner, images you capture or select are processed locally on your device using Google ML Kit Text Recognition to estimate tax withholding percentages. Your pay stub images are never uploaded to our servers.

4. Device Permissions

  • Camera / System Photo Picker: Used only when you choose to scan a pay stub photo.
  • Notifications: Used only if you enable optional daily shift reminders.
  • Biometrics: Used only if you enable the optional local Biometric Lock. Biometric verification is handled exclusively by your device's operating system.

5. PDF & CSV Exports

Pay summaries, paystubs, and CSV files are generated locally on your device and are only printed or shared when you explicitly initiate an export.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your payroll records are stored only on your device, you can delete individual records inside the app at any time or remove all local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Payroll Calculator.

7. Children's Privacy

Payroll Calculator is designed for general adult workforce use and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please email support@danglingdicestudios.com.
