Privacy policy & cookie compliance

1. No invasive trackers & data ethics

Dangling Dice Studios™ respects developer and creator privacy. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data to third-party data brokers. We do not embed behavioral retargeting pixels (such as Meta/Facebook Pixel or TikTok Pixel) or invasive cross-site ad networks.

2. Privacy-enhanced YouTube embedded media

Our video players stream via YouTube’s Privacy-Enhanced Mode (https://www.youtube-nocookie.com). In this mode, YouTube will not set tracking or advertising cookies on your browser simply for viewing our pages, and will only process necessary stream delivery once you actively hit Play.

3. Essential first-party cookies & local storage

We use strictly necessary first-party cookies and browser local storage for:

  • My Studio Stash: remembering conversions, sound kits and benchmarks you saved in this browser.
  • Consent state: remembering your acknowledgment of this privacy notice (dds_cookie_consent).
  • Admin authentication: secure encrypted HTTP session tokens for authenticated administrative portal access.

4. Affiliate programs & commercial transparency

Dangling Dice Studios™ participates in affiliate marketing programs, including Amazon Associates and Impact.com. Clicking partner recommendations uses clean, encrypted outbound referral links at zero additional cost to you.

5. GDPR (EU/UK) & CCPA/CPRA (California) rights

Under GDPR and CCPA, you have the right to request information on any data submitted through our forms (such as App Showcase submissions), or request immediate deletion by contacting our administrative team directly on Discord or via GitHub.
